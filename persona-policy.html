The problem

Research that resets is research that wastes.

Every policy inquiry spans months. Every session with a generic AI tool starts from zero. By the time you're writing the final brief, half your early findings are buried in folders, stale in chat histories, or simply forgotten.

Problem 01
Pre-publication material can't go to the cloud
Embargoed findings, unpublished datasets, working drafts — none of it should leave your institution's perimeter. Cloud-first tools create a compliance problem before you've written a word.
Problem 02
LLM confidence scores are meaningless
Research confirms that model-verbalized confidence is fundamentally broken — 90% stated confidence can mean 24% actual accuracy. Policy briefs built on uncalibrated confidence are liability, not intelligence.
Problem 03
Contradictions get smoothed, not surfaced
When two Tier 1 sources directly disagree, generic tools produce a paragraph that averages them. Distilapp flags the contradiction structurally — it's a first-class finding, not a footnote.
Problem 04
No audit trail for what the AI knew
A parliamentary committee or peer reviewer asks: what was the evidence basis for this recommendation? Generic tools have no answer. Distilapp's Decision Log records the full evidence state at the moment of every decision.
How it works for you

From inquiry to defensible brief.

On confidence and quality The brief appears immediately after each run. The app then tells you specifically what to review — which claims are too weak to include, where sources conflict. Ingest your institution's reports and mark them Tier 1: they contribute directly to confidence scores, outweighing LLM guesses. Pre-publication documents never leave your machine.
01

Pre-loaded with Tier 1 policy sources

Distilapp ships pre-configured with RAND, Brookings, IMF working papers, UN research feeds, OECD data, and arXiv policy papers — refreshed daily by a local model at zero API cost. When you start an inquiry, you're starting from a curated corpus of institutional research, already scored by credibility tier.

02

Evidence-weighted confidence — not model opinion

Every finding is scored from source tier, multi-provider consensus, Socratic challenge survival, document corroboration, and recency decay. An OECD report corroborating a claim is weighted more heavily than a news article. The scoring is deterministic and fully transparent.

03

Contradictions surface — they don't disappear

When the IMF and World Bank disagree on a structural reform finding, Distilapp doesn't average them. It flags the contradiction with both sources named, the specific disagreement described, and a contested confidence penalty applied. Your brief can address the disagreement explicitly — which is what peer reviewers actually need.

04

Policy brief — in your institution's structure

The Publish flow generates a structured policy brief from your evidence base. Paste your institution's template — Distilapp infers the section structure, heading style, and citation conventions and replicates them. Chatham House format, Brookings format, parliamentary brief, or your own structure.

05

Research Further — without resetting

When SNR or breadth is low, hit Research Further. Distilapp targets only the contested claims, unfilled gaps, and low-confidence findings. New findings merge into the existing graph. The brief you write in month three is built on everything you learned in months one and two.

06

Decision Log — the audit trail reviewers and committees now expect

When you finalise a policy position, log the decision. Distilapp captures every claim, its confidence score, the active contradictions, and the knowledge gaps at that moment. Timestamped, immutable, exportable. If a reviewer asks six months later, you have the exact answer.
