Policy is built on evidence.
The uncertainty belongs in the record too.

A policy brief can contain real citations and still overstate what the evidence supports. A single study can be repeated through many secondary sources. Evidence from one population can be silently generalised to another. A disputed claim can disappear into smooth synthesis.

Epistamate is designed to preserve the support, scope, contradictions and gaps underneath policy conclusions — together with the review history.

The structural problems

What gets lost when policy research
is compressed into a final brief.

Source lineage

Apparent consensus can be one original result amplified through many papers, reports and commentary. Counting sources is not enough; independence matters.

Applicability

Evidence can be strong and still fail to transfer to the jurisdiction, population, mechanism or implementation context of the policy question.

Contradiction

Disagreement is often professionally inconvenient, so synthesis resolves it too early. Epistamate treats contradiction and qualification as information rather than defects.

Unknowns

What the evidence does not establish may be more consequential than what it does. Gaps should survive the report and become future research targets.

Review provenance

A future reviewer should be able to distinguish AI-proposed priorities from claims an analyst actually inspected and judged.

Institutional memory

Prior research should be reusable with provenance and revalidation rather than rebuilt from scratch or copied forward without checking recency and applicability.

What Epistamate is built to add

A policy evidence record
that can be challenged later.

01

Claim-to-evidence traceability

Consequential propositions remain linked to source identity and the material that supports, qualifies or contradicts them. A bibliography is part of the output; the support chain is the more important object underneath it.

Research standard: reference exists → source supports claim → independent corroboration → evidence sufficient for this use.
02

Typed gaps and contradictions

Unresolved needs remain visible and can drive Research Further. The system is allowed to say “the evidence is insufficient” rather than manufacture a complete-looking brief.

Fail-closed: zero strict findings is a valid research result when the evidence does not warrant them.
03

AI-assisted planning without AI authority

Frontier models can improve acquisition planning, review prioritisation and adversarial challenge. Their suggestions remain advisory; model agreement does not count as independent evidence.

Practical benefit: use frontier intelligence to search more intelligently without letting fluent synthesis decide what is established.
04

Cumulative evidence with revalidation

The long-term Knowledge layer is intended to carry prior evidence, gaps and review history forward while checking currency, applicability and contradiction for each new policy question.

Current boundary: same-topic continuation and lineage exist; durable cross-topic Knowledge is still being rebuilt and is not claimed as a finished early-access feature.
05

Reviewer accountability

Researcher decisions are explicit events tied to the research state. A senior reviewer can reconstruct what evidence was available and what the analyst actually did, without treating approval as evidence strength.

Governance distinction: evidence authority, model reasoning and human judgment remain separate.
Domain policy

Policy research needs different rules
from generic web research.

Authority and jurisdiction

Official instruments, implementation evidence, expert analysis and commentary can play different roles. The engine architecture supports domain policy packs rather than relying on prompt instructions alone.

Mechanism and feasibility

A policy claim can be normatively attractive yet weakly evidenced on implementation feasibility or stakeholder effects. Those dimensions should remain separable.

Contested assumptions

Assumptions and transferability conditions should remain visible so a reviewer can challenge them instead of inheriting them from the synthesis.

Early-access boundary: General is the current gold path. Gov/Policy pack infrastructure exists, but live domain calibration remains a later milestone; this page describes the product direction without claiming full policy-production readiness.
