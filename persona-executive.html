Forty sources repeating one result
are not forty independent reasons to believe it.

Research quality depends on what the sources actually establish, whether corroboration is genuinely independent, which qualifications survive synthesis, and whether a later reviewer can reconstruct how the conclusion was formed.

Why the workflow is different

Evidence quality should be
inspectable, not implied.

Support, not citation presence

A bibliography can be technically correct while the source does not support the sentence attached to it. Epistamate treats the claim–evidence relationship as an inspectable object.

Independence, not volume

Repeated reporting of one underlying study or claim is amplification. Source/work/family identity is part of the evidence posture.

Unknowns remain visible

Gaps and contradictions should survive into the output and the next research plan instead of disappearing behind fluent synthesis.

Reviewer provenance

A senior reviewer should be able to see what AI prioritised, what the analyst inspected and which decisions were explicitly made.

If your team does consequential research that needs to survive informed review,

We are looking for workflows where source support, provenance, researcher judgement and repeatability matter more than faster prose.
