Evidence-led Advisory · Service · Internal decision-evidence workbench

A recommendation can be reasonable today.
Can you still reconstruct why six months later?

Consequential consulting work rarely starts with a clean evidence pack. It starts with sponsor claims, partial documents, meeting notes, public research, specialist input and unresolved assumptions — then changes as the engagement learns more.

The advisory method keeps the decision case structured while the evidence changes, without turning the client engagement into a software implementation project.

The engagement problem

Evidence arrives messy.
The decision still needs a controlled basis.

Capture first

Useful material is not always clean evidence.

Meeting recollections, emails, WhatsApp-derived notes, sponsor material and informal administrative intelligence can be captured without silently promoting them into fact. Original documents remain distinguishable from derived interpretation.

Structure the case

Claims and recommendations are separate objects.

Material claims can be supported, contradicted or left unresolved. Recommendations explicitly depend on claims, so an upstream change can reopen the recommendation instead of leaving stale advice looking current.

Stay current

New evidence should trigger reassessment, not revisionist history.

Point-in-time report snapshots and versioned state make it possible to answer what changed since the last circulated view and why the current recommendation differs.

Intake → Claims → Evidence / public research → Assessment → Recommendations → Controlled outputs → Updates / reassessment
What the internal workbench protects

The software is not the service.
The evidence discipline is.

Evidence authority

Assertion can stay useful without becoming fact.

A plain authority ladder keeps binding/legal, official, primary commercial/technical, credible external and unverified intelligence distinct. Lower-authority material can guide diligence while remaining visibly provisional.

Research boundary

Public research can shape relevance without proving client facts.

External research can explain why a prospect may care, reveal comparators or sharpen a test. The private evidence case determines what can safely be said about the client opportunity.

Model boundary

AI helps structure and challenge; humans own the judgement.

Private analysis packages can be abstracted so raw client evidence does not need to be exposed to a frontier model. Returned analysis remains candidate reasoning requiring consultant review.

Target-specific outputs

A pitch cannot become more confident than the underlying case.

Prospect- or audience-specific wording is derived from the shared evidence case. If a linked claim weakens, the external-facing statement should become qualified or held rather than remaining “verified”.

Dependency visibility

Recommendations have reasons that can go stale.

When source material, a material claim or a key assessment changes, dependent recommendations can be marked for review instead of silently persisting as if nothing changed.

Auditability

The reviewer can reconstruct what happened.

The durable record distinguishes source intake, model suggestions, consultant decisions, accepted/rejected external research and the evidence state behind a circulated output.

Due diligence / opportunity cases

Where the decision matures through documents, market evidence, expert input, counterparty feedback and unresolved diligence questions, the evidence-decision pattern helps preserve what each recommendation depends on.

Research-heavy advisory

Policy, regulatory, technology or market recommendations often mix external research with client-specific facts. The method keeps public evidence, applicability judgements and client evidence separate.

What this page does not claim

This is not an automated audit, a generic engagement-management platform, legal/tax/engineering/environmental judgement, or a team/cloud/SaaS product. The current workbench is an internal tool used to test the evidence-decision method on real engagement shapes. Client work product remains professional judgement, not software output.
