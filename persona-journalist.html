The problem

Investigations that outgrow
your note-taking system.

A six-month investigation generates hundreds of documents, dozens of sources, and a tangle of claims that confirm, contradict, and complicate each other. Spreadsheets and notebooks don't find contradictions. AI chat tools don't remember last month.

Problem 01

Source confidentiality requires local-only

Sensitive sources, unpublished documents, leaked material — none of it belongs on a remote server. Full stop. Distilapp is a desktop application. Everything stays on your machine. No cloud sync, no vendor access, no data retention policy to worry about.

Problem 02

Contradictions are buried in volume

An official says X. A document from three months earlier shows Y. A source from a different country confirms Z. Connecting these manually across a months-long investigation is the hardest part of the work. Distilapp tracks subject-verb-object triplets across all your sources and flags when they conflict.

Problem 03

The story's evidence needs to be defensible

Publication triggers legal review. Defamation defence requires proving what you knew, from which sources, and when. Distilapp's Decision Log records the exact evidence state at any point in the investigation — the immutable record that demonstrates what was known when the story was filed.

Problem 04

Evidence evaporates between sessions

Pick up an investigation after three weeks and you're re-reading notes, re-finding documents, re-establishing context. Distilapp's knowledge graph persists. The entity constellation from day one is still there on day ninety — with everything new connected to everything old.

How Distilapp works for you

From documents
to story graph.

Ingest documents, official statements, source interviews, public records. Contradictions surface structurally. The knowledge graph shows you how entities connect across your entire body of evidence.

01

Ingest everything — documents, URLs, PDFs, filings

Drop in government filings, corporate reports, leaked documents, official statements, court records, academic research. Distilapp's stratified chunking handles 300-page PDFs — always indexing the start, structural sections, and tail, so nothing important gets missed regardless of where it sits in a long document.

02

Story graph — entities, relationships, conflicts

Every entity in your evidence base — person, organisation, document, event — appears as a node in the knowledge graph. Edges connect them by subject-verb-object relationships extracted from every source. Click any entity to see everything Distilapp knows about it across your entire investigation. Contradictions between sources on the same entity are highlighted structurally.

03

Contradiction detection — when sources conflict

When an official statement claims entity A did X, and a document in your KB shows entity A did the opposite, Distilapp flags it as a contested claim with both sources named. Not keyword matching — triplet-based: same subject, opposing predicate object. The kind of contradiction that's the story gets surfaced automatically.

04

Source conflict highlighting

Which sources agree? Which sources are sole-source claims? Which findings only have a single document behind them versus multiple independent corroborations? The Claims tab shows you the evidence architecture of your investigation — not just the claims, but the reliability structure underneath them.

05

Decision Log — the defamation defence record

Before filing the story, log the decision. Distilapp records every verified finding, every contested claim, every source, every gap that remained — timestamped, immutable, exportable. If legal challenge follows publication, you have the complete record of what you knew, what was contested, and what you were transparent about not knowing.
