The problem in plain language

A polished AI-assisted answer can still fail
in six different ways.

Before you need an evidence architecture, you usually notice one of these practical problems.

Does the source support the claim?A real citation can still be attached to a claim it does not establish.
Are these really independent sources?Ten articles may all trace back to one study, dataset or announcement.
Does this apply to our case?A valid result may not transfer to your jurisdiction, population, workflow or implementation context.
What contradicts or qualifies it?A fluent synthesis can smooth away disagreement, caveats and alternative explanations.
What did a person actually review?“Human in the loop” means little if nobody can reconstruct what was inspected and decided.
What changed later?New evidence can weaken, strengthen or stale earlier conclusions without making that change obvious.
A controlled research run

Twenty-seven citations did not mean
twenty-seven usable evidence points.

A supplied industry fraud/cyber paper looked well-supported on the surface. Epistamate treated each step separately instead of assuming that a working link meant a supported claim.

What happened

The bibliography looked stronger than the inspectable evidence.

The run parsed 27 citations. Ten routes were reachable; 17 failed or were blocked. After preserving and de-duplicating what could actually be obtained, 11 unique source contents remained for analysis.

What Epistamate preserved

A reachable citation was still not automatically treated as support.

Route reachability, citation identity, claim support and substantive validity remained separate questions. Duplicate source content was not counted as independent corroboration, and unresolved support questions survived into the research record rather than disappearing into fluent prose.

Common questions

What people usually want to know
before going deeper.

Is Epistamate software or a service?

Both, but they are distinct. The Governed Research Workspace is the current software product in early access. GenAI Workflow Assurance and Consulting & Advisory are bounded service applications that use the same evidence-control architecture.

Does Epistamate replace ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini Deep Research?

No. Epistamate is designed to use strong frontier models for search, reading, reasoning and challenge. Its role is to preserve the evidence, unresolved state and human decisions around that model-assisted work.

What does “local-first” mean?

In the current Research Workspace, canonical research state is stored locally. External model or web assistance is an explicit step rather than an invisible authority path. The exact privacy boundary depends on which external services you choose to use.

Can I use confidential material?

The architecture is designed around local research state and bounded external assistance, but you should not send confidential material to an external model unless your own policy and provider terms allow it. Closed-source and more tightly bounded workflows remain an important product direction.

What does “human authority” actually mean?

Models can propose plans, interpretations, challenges and drafts. A human decides what to accept, qualify, defer, reject or carry forward. That decision is recorded, but human approval does not make weak evidence stronger.

When is Epistamate probably unnecessary?

For brainstorming, casual research or low-consequence drafting, the extra evidence discipline may be more overhead than value. It is aimed at work where someone may later ask: what supported this, what was uncertain, and who decided?
