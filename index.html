Keep new possibilities grounded as the work continues

What changed?See the material differences since the last review, not just another inventory of recent information.
What still holds?Reuse earlier assessments and supporting reasons, checking whether new information actually changes their applicability.
What is unresolved?Preserve open commitments, conflicting evidence and uncertainty instead of smoothing them out of the next summary.
What needs attention?Bring affected diagnoses, risks, recommendations or deliverable sections to the professional—not a wall of new items.
What can the next deliverable use?Prepare possible trends, insights and talking points from maintained context in familiar organizational formats.
Who decides?Frontier AI suggests; the responsible professional determines what is established, what to recommend and what to communicate.

Common questions

What people usually want to know
before going deeper.

Is Epistamate software or a service?

The Governed Research Workspace is the current software product in early access. GenAI Workflow Assurance is an offered service; Consulting & Advisory has a controlled method-aware experiment. Other workflow expressions are possible adaptations rather than off-the-shelf products.

Does Epistamate replace ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or specialist research systems?

No. Frontier AI supplies discovery, interpretation, challenge and synthesis, and that leverage can evolve as models improve. Epistamate maintains evidence-bearing analytical continuity beyond an individual model answer.

Is Epistamate one universal workflow for every domain?

No. The shared epistemic foundation preserves provenance, uncertainty, accumulated state, dependencies, change and professional authority. Source authority and trust tiers, methodology, quick-pass or detailed inspection, vocabulary and deliverable formats are tailored to each workflow.

Does Epistamate calculate a confidence score?

No. Epistamate does not calculate a universal confidence score or source-independence score. Directness, source role, exact support, independence, applicability, contradiction, recency and sufficiency remain separate, inspectable dimensions rather than being collapsed into one number.

How does Epistamate decide what deserves attention?

It should not rely on a single opaque score. Attention can be driven by inspectable state such as contradiction, weak or sparse support, applicability uncertainty, missing documents or facts, stale downstream work, researcher priority or newly arrived evidence. The researcher should be able to see why an item surfaced, what evidence/state caused it, what it affects and what action could resolve it.

Does every workflow end in the same kind of report?

No. Durable analytical state underpins the next set of useful insights or deliverables in formats familiar to each workflow. Research briefs, advisory recommendations, market assessments and account reviews are examples with different implementation maturity.

What does “local-first” mean?

In the current Research Workspace, canonical research state, review decisions and locally supplied evidence are stored on the user’s machine. Explicit web acquisition or external-model operations may send the material required for that operation to the configured external service. Local-first does not mean zero-network.

Can I use confidential material?

The architecture is designed around local research state and bounded external assistance, but you should not send confidential material to an external model unless your own policy and provider terms allow it. Closed-source and more tightly bounded workflows remain an important product direction.

What does “human authority” actually mean?

Models can propose plans, interpretations, challenges and drafts. A human decides what to accept, qualify, defer, reject or carry forward. That decision is recorded, but human approval does not make weak evidence stronger.

When is Epistamate probably unnecessary?

For isolated research questions, casual drafting or one-off analyses without lasting reasoning or consequential reassessment, a simpler tool may be preferable. Epistamate is designed for work that continues, accumulates and may need a defensible account of what changed.

Does Epistamate automatically refresh research or rewrite recommendations?

An optional governed research corpus could supply relevant external developments alongside ordinary workflow inputs. Universal autonomous monitoring and packaged account-management refresh are not current features. New evidence can prompt impact assessment; it does not independently authorise a changed professional judgement.
