The problem

AI gave you more answers.
It gave you less certainty.

The information keeps coming. The confidence keeps dropping. Here's why.

01

Everything is plausible, nothing is provable

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Deep Research — all return fluent sentences with no indication of what's verified, contested, or simply fabricated. You spend more time checking than deciding.

02

Every session forgets everything

Research you did last Tuesday is invisible today. There's no accumulation, no linkage between topics, no way to ask "what do I already know about this?" Knowledge evaporates.

03

One output, regardless of what you need

An analyst, an executive, and a first-year student all need the same facts framed completely differently. A single chat output serves none of them well.

04

No audit trail for your decisions

When a choice is challenged six months later, you have no record of what you knew, how confident you were, or what contradictions you were aware of at the time.

05

Your specialist models have nowhere to go

You've fine-tuned a model on your legal corpus, clinical data, or financial filings. Every AI tool forces you to use their models. Your fine-tune has no role in the pipeline.

How Distila works

Four phases. One growing intelligence layer.

Every research run goes through the same rigorous pipeline — then adds to a private knowledge graph that compounds over time. When coverage is thin, the pipeline detects it and reformulates the query automatically before moving forward.

Phase 1

Discovery scan

Distila quickly maps the terrain — themes, initial claims, and knowledge gaps — using your own LLM providers in parallel. Web grounding optional, always transparent.

Phase 2

Deep research

Multiple AI providers work in parallel. Claims appear live as each provider responds — the Claims counter increments in real time. Every claim carries a status (Verified, Contested, Weak), an evidence-weighted confidence score, a source tier, and the full reasoning chain.

Phase 3

Socratic challenge

The system interrogates its own findings. What assumptions underpin each claim? Where does the evidence actually conflict? What would have to be false for this conclusion to hold?

Phase 4

Synthesis + publication

Orchestrator synthesises the brief through your chosen decision lens. A dedicated Report Writer model produces publication-ready output — board paper, investment memo, blog post, LinkedIn thread, or policy brief — in the prose style you trust.

Decision lenses

Same research, different frame

Switch between Analyst, Executive, Financial, and other lenses to reframe the same evidence base for different audiences — without re-running research. Build custom lenses for your own use case. The guided lens builder writes the synthesis prompt for you.

Research further

Targeted iteration

When confidence is low, hit Research Further. Distila targets contested claims, known gaps, and low-confidence findings — only those — then merges new claims back into your graph without resetting. Each run compounds on the last. Choose ×1, ×2, or ×3 passes.

Always on

Knowledge graph

Every claim, entity, and document persists in a private local database. The Graph tab renders a live constellation — nodes sized by mention frequency, edges from subject–verb–object triplets. Click any entity to see everything Distila knows about it across all your research. Contradictions surface structurally, not by keyword matching.

Evidence weighted

Confidence you can trust

Confidence is computed from evidence — source tier, P(True)-weighted provider consensus, Socratic challenge survival, document corroboration, and recency decay. The contested penalty is tier-aware: a Tier 1 source challenged by another Tier 1 source is penalised more heavily than a Tier 3 challenge. LLM verbalized confidence is fundamentally broken. Distila doesn't use it.

Positions

Stake and track

State what you believe to be true. Every subsequent research run checks whether new claims support or challenge your position. The confidence bar moves. You see the evidence shift in real time.

Topic hierarchy

Research trees

Create sub-topics under any parent topic — drill into a specific angle without losing the broader context. When sub-topics have their own briefs, a single button synthesises them all into a cross-cutting parent brief that identifies where the threads agree, conflict, and diverge.

Your data

Fully private — fully yours

Distila runs on your machine. The knowledge graph lives in a local database. AI providers process queries but never store your research. Fine-tune a domain specialist, add it as a worker, run fully airgapped. Your models, your data, your pipeline.

The configurable pipeline

Your models. Your roles.
Different models for different jobs.

Distila assigns a specific role to each model — so the best model for research isn't forced to write, and the best model for writing isn't wasted on verification. Mix any combination of cloud, local, and fine-tuned models. Our philosophy
Responsible AI by design
AI should do the heavy lifting.
Humans should own every conclusion.

Responsible AI isn't a compliance checkbox. It's an architecture decision. Distila is built ground-up with the human governance layer that almost every AI tool skips. The confidence score on each claim isn't the model's self-assessment — research from 2025-2026 confirms LLM verbalized confidence is fundamentally broken. Distila computes confidence from evidence: source tier, P(True)-weighted provider consensus, verification outcome, Socratic challenge survival, and recency decay. When two Tier 1 sources directly contradict each other, the penalty is steeper than when a low-credibility source challenges a high-credibility one. The field calls this calibrated behavioural validation. Distila has had the foundations since day one and keeps sharpening them.
