How Distilapp works

Conviction in.
Verified fact out.

Four phases that turn what you believe into what you can defend — then persist it, compound it, and record it permanently. The source library keeps the evidence base fresh between runs, at zero API cost.

Phase 1

Discovery scan

Distilapp quickly maps the terrain — themes, initial claims, and knowledge gaps — using your own LLM providers in parallel. Web grounding optional, always transparent.

Phase 2

Deep research

Multiple AI providers work in parallel. Claims appear live as each provider responds — the Claims counter increments in real time. Every claim carries a status (Verified, Contested, Weak), an evidence-weighted confidence score, a source tier, and the full reasoning chain.

Phase 3

Socratic challenge

The system interrogates its own findings. What assumptions underpin each claim? Where does the evidence actually conflict? What would have to be false for this conclusion to hold?

Sufficiency gate

Abstain rather than confabulate

Before synthesis, Distilapp checks whether the evidence base is sufficient to support a reliable brief. If coverage is too thin — too few sources, too many gaps, too much contestation — the pipeline abstains and tells you why, rather than generating a confident-sounding brief on weak foundations. Most tools don't have this gate. Distilapp does.

Phase 4

Synthesis + publication

Orchestrator synthesises the brief through your chosen decision lens. A dedicated Report Writer model produces publication-ready output — board paper, investment memo, blog post, LinkedIn thread, or policy brief — in the prose style you trust.

Decision log

Record what you knew

When you act on research, log the decision. Distilapp captures the full evidence state at that moment — every claim, its confidence score, the contradictions you were aware of, and the gaps that remained. Immutable, timestamped, audit-ready. The record regulators and boards now require.

Decision lenses

Same research, different frame

Switch between Analyst, Executive, Financial, and other lenses to reframe the same evidence base for different audiences — without re-running research. Build custom lenses for your own use case. The guided lens builder writes the synthesis prompt for you.

Research further

Targeted iteration

When confidence is low, hit Research Further. Distilapp targets contested claims, known gaps, and low-confidence findings — only those — then merges new claims back into your graph without resetting. Each run compounds on the last. Choose ×1, ×2, or ×3 passes.

Always on

Knowledge graph

Every claim, entity, and document persists in a private local database. The Graph tab renders a live constellation — nodes sized by mention frequency, edges from subject–verb–object triplets. Click any entity to see everything Distilapp knows about it across all your research. Contradictions surface structurally, not by keyword matching.

Evidence weighted

Confidence you can trust

Confidence is computed from evidence — source tier, provider consensus weighting, Socratic challenge survival, document corroboration, and recency decay. The contested penalty is tier-aware: a Tier 1 source challenged by another Tier 1 source is penalised more heavily than a Tier 3 challenge. LLM verbalized confidence is fundamentally broken. Distilapp doesn't use it.

Adaptive UI

Switch between Basic and Advanced mode. Basic mode shows SNR score, top findings, contested claims flagged, and the Decision Log button — nothing else. Advanced mode exposes full pipeline controls, provider toggles, confidence breakdowns, and graph depth. Focus mode reduces everything to the centre pane. The same research, adapted to how you actually work.

Positions

Topic hierarchy

Research trees

Create sub-topics under any parent topic — drill into a specific angle without losing the broader context. When sub-topics have their own briefs, a single button synthesises them all into a cross-cutting parent brief that identifies where the threads agree, conflict, and diverge.

Source library

Always-fresh, always curated

Distilapp ships pre-configured with high-credibility public sources — RAND, IMF, BIS, NBER working papers, Brookings, arXiv, UN research feeds. Responsible AI · Our philosophy

Responsible AI isn't a compliance checkbox. It's an architecture decision. Distilapp is built ground-up with the human governance layer that almost every AI tool skips. Distilapp computes confidence from evidence: source tier, provider consensus weighting, verification outcome, Socratic challenge survival, and recency decay. When two Tier 1 sources directly contradict each other, the penalty is steeper than when a low-credibility source challenges a high-credibility one. The EU AI Act's full enforcement in 2026 made explicit what Distilapp was built to do from day one: high-stakes AI decisions require documented evidence of what the system knew and how calibrated its confidence was. Distilapp's architecture is designed to support those human oversight workflows — the Decision Log, the sufficiency gate, the audit trail — built in, not bolted on.
