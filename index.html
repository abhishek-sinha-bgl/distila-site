Where the distinction actually is

“Evidence” and “auditability” are not enough.
The unit of control matters.

Research agents, scholarly evidence tools and governance platforms already solve important parts of the problem. Epistamate is aimed at the interface between a consequential proposition and the authority you are willing to give it.

Frontier research agents

Excellent cognition

Search, read, reason and draft at a level Epistamate should exploit rather than imitate. The remaining problem is durable evidentiary state and review authority.

Scholarly evidence tools

Deep literature workflows

Strong paper discovery, sentence-level grounding and systematic-review mechanics. Epistamate targets mixed professional evidence and continuing decision state beyond literature alone.

AI governance platforms

Strong governance systems of record

Inventory, policy, risk, controls, testing and audit artifacts. Epistamate asks the narrower proposition-level question: what does this evidence establish about the control in this actual workflow?

The controls underneath the prose

A polished answer can hide six
different evidence failures.

SupportDoes the cited/acquired material actually support the exact proposition?
LineageAre apparently different sources one underlying work, dataset or evidence family?
ApplicabilityDoes a real finding transfer to this population, jurisdiction, configuration or workflow?
ChallengeWhat contradicts, qualifies or offers a plausible alternative explanation?
ReviewWhat did AI propose, and what did the researcher actually inspect and decide?
ChangeWhat became stale, weakened or stronger when later evidence or context changed?
frontier intelligence proposes → Epistamate constrains / verifies / records → researcher authorises consequential transitions

Research

Early-access General path

Planning and permissions, live acquisition, configured semantic assessment, evidence/gap/relationship surfaces, researcher review infrastructure, audit history, continuation controls and outputs are implemented. End-to-end usefulness is still being validated on real runs.

Assurance

Working workflow-assurance method

Workflow propositions, operational evidence, research-applicability, assessment, dependency and reassessment machinery support the service. It is not presented as an autonomous audit or certification system.

Compounding knowledge

Lineage now; cross-topic reuse next

Same-topic continuation and reassessment are real. Durable cross-topic canonical Knowledge with freshness/applicability revalidation remains a product milestone, so the site does not claim it as complete.
