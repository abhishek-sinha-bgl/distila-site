How it works · Governed reasoning framework

Frontier intelligence can propose.
Evidence still has to earn authority.

Epistamate is built around a separation that becomes more important as models improve: model reasoning, governed evidence state and professional judgement are not the same thing.

Use the strongest available intelligence for cognition. Keep source identity, representation, model-facing attention, human authority and later change explicit in application-owned state.

ATTENTION · EXPLAINABLE

Do not just rank. Preserve the reason.

An attention item should remain connected to the state signal that surfaced it, the evidence behind it, what is still unresolved, the downstream work it could affect and the next action that could resolve it. This is different from an opaque score or a generic “human in the loop” queue.

PRIMARY DOCUMENT · CURRENT BUILD

Full represented-unit processing recovered a known missed term.

In a fresh supplier-terms run, all 76 represented primary-document passages were processed. A 48-hour incident-notification provision missed by an earlier selective run surfaced and survived into synthesis. This is one known-positive recovery, not a selector-recall measure or claim of legal completeness.

RESEARCHER-OWNED EVIDENCE · CURRENT BUILD

Must-consider did not mean must-believe.

A researcher-owned automotive dataset was required for consideration without gaining automatic authority. All 14 represented units received model-facing consideration; later context made one earlier model proposal stale, and the researcher rejected it.

SUFFICIENCY · INTENDED USE

Enough for this use did not mean research complete.

The automotive run recorded a researcher judgement that the state was sufficient for a qualified advisory assessment while preserving the unresolved evidence that still blocked a specific import, assembly, localisation or export recommendation.

OUTPUT · PURPOSE-SHAPED

The deliverable is a projection of state.

The same accepted analytical state can project into different professional outputs without changing the evidence and judgement beneath it. Current runs exercised distinct advisory, findings, policy-analysis and senior-official forms from governed state.

ADVISORY WORKFLOW

New evidence made downstream work stale, not silently rewritten.

In a synthetic specialist-advisory experiment, accepted evidence propagated through working propositions, diagnostics, findings, recommendations and affected deliverables in causal order while the consultant retained authority at each transition.

Governance kernel

The contribution is not one technique.
It is the architecture around changing state.

01 · ADDRESSABLE CLAIMS

Control below the report level.

Consequential propositions remain durable objects that can be supported, qualified, contradicted, reviewed and reused independently of the prose around them.

02 · EVIDENCE POSTURE

No magic confidence number.

Directness, source role, exact support, independence, applicability, contradiction, recency and sufficiency remain inspectable dimensions.

Epistamate does not calculate a universal confidence score or source-independence score. Evidence posture remains multidimensional and inspectable rather than collapsed into a single number.

03 · ADVERSARIAL CHALLENGE

Try to break the conclusion.

Models can surface counterevidence and alternative explanations, but challenge changes canonical posture only when the evidence justifies it.

04 · PERSISTENT GAPS

Unknowns survive synthesis.

Missing evidence is a durable research object and possible Research Further target, not a disclaimer that disappears after drafting.

05 · CUMULATIVE STATE

Reuse without laundering old answers.

Inherited evidence can be reconsidered for freshness, applicability and contradiction. Inherited answer authority is not automatically reusable.

06 · BIDIRECTIONAL OPERATION

Question-led and source-led work share standards.

A research question can drive acquisition; a supplied report can drive verification. Both enter the same evidence and review spine.

Authority model

Three kinds of authority.
Never silently collapsed.

MODEL / REASONING

Can propose.

Search lanes, interpretations, triage, challenge and drafts. Model agreement can improve process robustness; it is not independent evidence.

EVIDENCE

Must be earned.

Acquired source identity, exact support, evidence function, work/family lineage, independence, scope and other domain gates determine what a proposition can claim.

RESEARCHER

Can judge and authorise.

Human review is essential governance. It records acceptance, rejection, deferral and continuation authority without pretending that approval strengthened the source evidence.
