One evidence discipline.
Different consequential workflows.

Research, assurance and advisory work look different on the surface. Underneath, the same integrity problem recurs: assertions, source material, model reasoning, professional judgement and later changes must remain distinguishable.

The engine exists to preserve the chain from evidence to claim, judgement, recommendation and reassessment — while keeping model capability separate from professional and evidentiary authority.

Separate capability from authority. Use the strongest available intelligence for planning and reasoning. Require evidence, provenance and explicit review for consequential claims.

Six co-present properties

The contribution is not one technique.
It is the architecture around them.

The original six-principle thesis remains the architectural core. The canonical rebuild has replaced several early implementation mechanisms with stricter evidence and authority semantics.

01 · ADDRESSABLE CLAIMS

Research is represented below the report level.

Claims and propositions are durable, individually addressable objects. They can be traced to source/work identity and exact supporting material, reviewed independently, challenged, qualified, contradicted or carried forward without treating the whole report as one unit of truth.

02 · EVIDENCE POSTURE

Confidence is not a model feeling — or a magic number.

The engine evaluates what kind of evidence exists beneath a claim: directness, source function and authority, body/span availability, independence, applicability, contradiction, recency and sufficiency. A numeric summary may be useful later; it never replaces the underlying posture.

03 · ADVERSARIAL CHALLENGE

Strong research tries to break its own conclusions.

Challenge looks for counterevidence, alternative explanations, scope failures and missing assumptions before synthesis hardens into a recommendation. Frontier models can be powerful challengers, but challenge output remains advisory until evidence supports a canonical change.

04 · PERSISTENT GAPS

Unknowns are first-class research objects.

Gaps are not a disclaimer pasted onto the end of a report. They are typed, durable targets that survive synthesis, can be prioritised by the researcher and can drive Research Further when additional evidence would materially change the conclusion.

05 · CUMULATIVE RESEARCH STATE

Prior evidence can be reused without laundering old conclusions.

Research should compound across iterations and, ultimately, across topics. Reuse carries provenance and review history, then re-evaluates applicability, currency, contradiction and source validity for the new question. Inherited evidence is not inherited answer authority.

06 · BIDIRECTIONAL OPERATION

Question-led research and document-led verification share one substrate.

A research question can drive acquisition and synthesis; a supplied report can drive verification and challenge. Both directions use the same source identity, evidence, review and audit boundaries rather than separate prompt workflows with different standards.

+ DOMAIN POLICY

Evidence rules belong in policy packs, not prompt folklore.

Different domains need different source roles, authority classes, applicability tests and output structures. The canonical engine owns universal identity, lineage and authority mechanics; domain packs own the evidence policy that legitimately varies between General research, policy work and regulatory monitoring.

Authority model

Three kinds of authority must
never collapse into one another.

Model / reasoning capability

A model may propose search lanes, candidate interpretations, priority items, adversarial challenges or next-research targets. Multiple models agreeing can improve process robustness. It does not create independent evidentiary corroboration.

Evidence authority

Evidence authority comes from acquired and represented sources, exact support relationships, source function, independence, scope and other canonical gates. A secondary article repeating a primary study is not the same as having acquired the primary study.

Researcher authority

Researchers can review, accept, reject, defer, authorise continuation and record decisions. Those actions matter enormously for governance, but they do not directly strengthen source independence, support strength or confidence. Human approval is not evidence.

Evidence chain

A bibliography is necessary.
An auditable support chain is better.

The engine is designed around the claim–evidence interface: what exact source supports what exact proposition, in what role, under what conditions?

Reference exists ≠ source supports claim. A cited paper can be real and still fail to support the proposition attributed to it.
Topical relevance ≠ evidentiary relevance. Metadata, snippets and adjacent literature are useful leads, not evidence admission.
Two URLs ≠ two independent sources. Duplicate representations, syndicated material and sources sharing the same underlying work do not create corroboration.
Strong evidence somewhere ≠ applicable evidence here. Population, jurisdiction, time period, workflow and mechanism all matter to transferability.
Silence ≠ support. Missing evidence stays a gap. The engine is allowed to end with zero strict findings.
Institutional knowledge

Memory is not enough.
Knowledge needs provenance and revalidation.

Why long context is not the answer

A million-token context can expose old material to a model. It does not tell you which prior claims were actually supported, which were later contradicted, which are stale, or which were accepted without adequate review.

Why RAG is not the whole answer

Retrieval brings back documents or chunks. Epistamate's target is richer: claim identity + exact evidence + provenance + applicability + independence + review history + temporal validity + contradiction state + version lineage.

How reuse should work

A prior claim can be reused only after the new context is evaluated. The result may be strengthened, qualified, weakened, superseded or marked stale. Research compounds without pretending that yesterday's answer is automatically today's truth.

Reviewer governance

In high-stakes research,
who reviewed what is part of the evidence story.

AI proposal

The system records what AI assistance recommended — planning changes, triage priorities, interpretations or challenges — without disguising those recommendations as researcher decisions.

Researcher action

Review actions are explicit, durable events bound to the research checkpoint and target. A senior reviewer can distinguish inspection and judgment from passive acceptance of an AI-generated queue.

Evidence state at decision time

The point of an audit trail is not merely to know that someone clicked “accept.” It is to reconstruct what evidence, contradictions, gaps and qualifications were visible when the decision was made.

Integrity contracts around the six principles

The public architecture is stronger when it says
what the engine refuses to conflate.

Evidence ≠ assertion

A management statement, meeting note, sponsor slide or model output can be useful without being established fact.

Source presence ≠ support

A valid URL, DOI or bibliography entry does not establish that the source supports the proposition attached to it.

Model agreement ≠ verification

Multiple frontier models can converge on the same interpretation and still be wrong in the same direction.

Human approval ≠ stronger evidence

Human judgement is authoritative for professional decisions and issuance. It is recorded as provenance, not converted into evidentiary corroboration.

Research relevance ≠ client fact

External research can sharpen a challenge or test. It cannot prove that a specific client control operated or that a specific business claim is true.

Update ≠ overwrite

New evidence can stale an assessment or recommendation. Prior states remain reconstructable instead of being silently rewritten.

Canonical rebuild status

Principles are durable.
Capabilities still have to be earned.

The original prototype implemented several ideas with simpler scoring and graph mechanisms. The canonical rebuild is intentionally replacing those shortcuts with stricter evidence, identity and authority contracts.

Implemented / live validation

Current-run canonical research

General research currently exercises explicit planning/permissions, acquisition, source/evidence inspection, Semantic assessment, gaps/relationships, researcher review infrastructure, continuation controls, audit/activity history and evidence-bearing outputs. End-to-end live quality is still being tested and hardened.

Active development

Bounded frontier-model assistance

Planning and review assistance are being integrated behind typed, digest-bound contracts. Future stages include stronger adversarial challenge and Research Further advice while retaining zero evidence-authority delta from the assistant itself.

Canonical rebuild

Durable Knowledge / cross-topic compounding

Same-question iteration and follow-on lineage exist, but the durable cross-topic Knowledge layer and long-lived knowledge graph are not yet claimed as finished early-access capabilities. Their target semantics are provenance-first, versioned and recency/applicability aware.
