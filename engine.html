How it works · Governed reasoning framework

A shared foundation
for work that endures.

Imagine using what an organization has already learned to explore a promising new market question, a possible client opportunity or a changed strategic choice. Epistamate is designed to keep that understanding usable across successive investigations: preserving the supporting evidence, professional decisions, uncertainty and relationships so new work can build on what came before.

Research informs both sides of the foundation: stronger capabilities for discovering and testing insight, and safeguards that preserve source support, limitations and professional authority as the technology evolves.

Maintain the reasoning.
Revisit only what new information puts in question.

Epistamate's intended role is an enduring analytical layer between existing information systems and the professional's familiar deliverables. It preserves evidence, prior assessments, unresolved questions and decisions; makes changes and potential dependencies inspectable; and helps direct scarce professional attention toward consequential reassessment.

PERSISTENT STATE

Accumulation, not a report archive

Preserve what supported an earlier judgement, its limitations, its history and which diagnoses, recommendations or outputs depend on it.

OPTIONAL INTELLIGENCE FEED

Refresh from work and research

New client, operational or other professional material may arrive through ordinary work. A maintained research corpus could also supply relevant external developments. Research refresh is optional, not compulsory.

IMPACT AND ATTENTION

Focus on what matters

Find which existing positions and deliverables may be affected, why and where professional judgement is needed. Candidate impact does not automatically change accepted conclusions.

The aim is to support the next set of useful insights or deliverables without recreating the entire analytical chain. This is the cross-workflow architectural direction; end-to-end unattended refresh and all illustrated professional applications have not been implemented or validated.

One epistemic foundation · many professional methods

Stable integrity principles.
Trust and workflow rules tuned to the work.

The shared foundation retains evidence identity and provenance, uncertainty, accepted versus provisional state, version history, dependency-aware impact and human authority. Different professional applications should be able to invoke these capabilities selectively through stable interfaces, while defining their own concepts, source roles, review responsibilities and outputs. It does not prescribe one universal method.

AUTHORITY & TRUST

Source roles and tiers

A regulation, a client-confirmed commitment, a system SLA measure and a scholarly paper play different evidentiary roles. Authority, trust and applicability rules belong to the specific method and decision context.

METHOD & ATTENTION

Professional judgement paths

Use the organization's criteria, diagnostics, review responsibilities and preferred quick-pass or detailed-inspection posture. A quick system-provisional pass must not masquerade as human approval.

OUTPUTS

Familiar deliverables

Research papers, policy briefs, advisory recommendations, market assessments and account reviews are illustrative expressions of the same underlying state—not a catalogue of finished applications.

Common capabilities, different wrappers. A research variant may use source lineage and reviewed findings; an advisory workflow may use proposition dependencies and recommendation reviews; a future client-specific solution might also propose patterns across accounts and test their applicability. The architectural aim is reusable, API-like components beneath the domain workflow—not copies of another application or an agent making professional judgements autonomously.

Implementation maturity varies: shared integrity contracts and important within-workflow capabilities have been exercised, but a general callable cross-context knowledge graph, shared enterprise decision API and automated organizational transfer are not finished products. Research is the most complete current software expression; advisory has been exercised experimentally; market and account applications remain concepts.

Research-informed capability · Research-informed safeguards

Built to improve with the research.
Without losing what was established.

Epistamate is designed as an evolving analytical foundation, not a fixed set of prompts or a finished catalogue of AI applications. Research and controlled experiments inform what we try next: new ways to acquire, connect, challenge and synthesize evidence, alongside more reliable ways to preserve its provenance, uncertainty and professional review. The two must develop together.

ANALYTICAL CAPABILITY

Improve what the work can discover

Investigate stronger extraction, evidence coverage, reasoning, synthesis and context-reuse methods. Evaluate whether a change actually helps a professional task—not simply whether a model or technique is newer.

EVIDENCE & SAFEGUARDS

Improve how conclusions are warranted

Test exact source support, scope and applicability, contradictory material, decision authority and review burden. New analysis remains a proposal until the workflow's evidence and professional checks permit more.

CONTINUED EVOLUTION

Offer useful advances to different workflows

Develop reusable capabilities with explicit interfaces and compatibility checks, so a Research or Advisory expression can choose what to adopt. New methods should not silently rewrite the accepted reasoning behind earlier decisions.

This is a continuing engineering and research direction, supported by controlled implementation observations, not a claim of measured superiority over other systems or of a completed enterprise context graph. ATTENTION · EXPLAINABLE

Do not just rank. Preserve the reason.

An attention item should remain connected to the state signal that surfaced it, the evidence behind it, what is still unresolved, the downstream work it could affect and the next action that could resolve it. This is different from an opaque score or a generic “human in the loop” queue.

PRIMARY DOCUMENT · CURRENT BUILD

Full represented-unit processing recovered a known missed term.

In a fresh supplier-terms run, all 76 represented primary-document passages were processed. A 48-hour incident-notification provision missed by an earlier selective run surfaced and survived into synthesis. This is one known-positive recovery, not a selector-recall measure or claim of legal completeness.

RESEARCHER-OWNED EVIDENCE · CURRENT BUILD

Must-consider did not mean must-believe.

A researcher-owned automotive dataset was required for consideration without gaining automatic authority. All 14 represented units received model-facing consideration; later context made one earlier model proposal stale, and the researcher rejected it.

SUFFICIENCY · INTENDED USE

Enough for this use did not mean research complete.

The automotive run recorded a researcher judgement that the state was sufficient for a qualified advisory assessment while preserving the unresolved evidence that still blocked a specific import, assembly, localisation or export recommendation.

OUTPUT · PURPOSE-SHAPED

The deliverable is a projection of state.

The same accepted analytical state can project into different professional outputs without changing the evidence and judgement beneath it. Current runs exercised distinct advisory, findings, policy-analysis and senior-official forms from governed state.

ADVISORY WORKFLOW

New evidence made downstream work stale, not silently rewritten.

In a synthetic specialist-advisory experiment, accepted evidence propagated through working propositions, diagnostics, findings, recommendations and affected deliverables in causal order while the consultant retained authority at each transition.

Governance kernel

The contribution is not one technique.
It is the architecture around changing state.

01 · ADDRESSABLE CLAIMS

Control below the report level.

Consequential propositions remain durable objects that can be supported, qualified, contradicted, reviewed and reused independently of the prose around them.

02 · EVIDENCE POSTURE

No magic confidence number.

Directness, source role, exact support, independence, applicability, contradiction, recency and sufficiency remain inspectable dimensions.

Epistamate does not calculate a universal confidence score or source-independence score. Evidence posture remains multidimensional and inspectable rather than collapsed into a single number.

03 · ADVERSARIAL CHALLENGE

Try to break the conclusion.

Models can surface counterevidence and alternative explanations, but challenge changes canonical posture only when the evidence justifies it.

04 · PERSISTENT GAPS

Unknowns survive synthesis.

Missing evidence is a durable research object and possible Research Further target, not a disclaimer that disappears after drafting.

05 · CUMULATIVE STATE

Reuse without laundering old answers.

Inherited evidence can be reconsidered for freshness, applicability and contradiction. Inherited answer authority is not automatically reusable.

06 · BIDIRECTIONAL OPERATION

Question-led and source-led work share standards.

A research question can drive acquisition; a supplied report can drive verification. Both enter the same evidence and review spine.

Authority model

Three kinds of authority.
Never silently collapsed.

MODEL / REASONING

Can propose.

Search lanes, interpretations, triage, challenge and drafts. Model agreement can improve process robustness; it is not independent evidence.

EVIDENCE

Must be earned.

Acquired source identity, exact support, evidence function, work/family lineage, independence, scope and other domain gates determine what a proposition can claim.

RESEARCHER

Can judge and authorise.

Human review is essential governance. It records acceptance, rejection, deferral and continuation authority without pretending that approval strengthened the source evidence.
