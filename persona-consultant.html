The problem

Fast research that can't survive a challenge.

Generic AI tools produce fluent, confident answers. They don't tell you which sources conflict, which findings are contested, or whether the confidence is real. That's fine for a draft. It's a liability in client delivery.

Problem 01
Confidence with no basis
An AI says "revenue growth is accelerating." At what confidence? From which sources? Contested by whom? Without answers, you're relaying the model's opinion, not evidence.
Problem 02
Every engagement starts from scratch
Your firm has done three engagements in the same sector. Every time, research starts over. Institutional knowledge evaporates. Distilapp's knowledge graph persists — sector intelligence compounds engagement by engagement.
Problem 03
Client confidentiality on cloud tools
NDA'd materials, client data, sensitive financials — none of it should be processed on a remote server with opaque data retention policies. Distilapp runs locally. Nothing leaves the machine.
Problem 04
No audit trail when scope is questioned
Six months after delivery, the client asks what you knew when you made that recommendation. Generic tools have nothing. Distilapp's Decision Log has everything — timestamped, immutable, exportable.
How it works for you

From brief to billable deliverable.

01

Stake the research question — not just a topic

Distilapp works from specific research questions, not vague topics. "What is the regulatory risk profile for fintech lending in the EU in 2026?" produces targeted, scoped findings. The pipeline phases focus on what's actually relevant to the engagement question.

02

Every finding is a structured claim with a score

Not a paragraph. A typed object: status (Verified / Contested / Weak), confidence percentage, source tier, provider consensus count, citations. When a partner asks "how confident are we in this?" you have a precise, defensible answer.

03

Contested findings are flagged — not hidden

Conflicting analyst forecasts, divergent regulatory interpretations, competing market size estimates — Distilapp surfaces them as contested claims with both sides documented. You decide how to handle the conflict in the deliverable. You're not blindsided by it in the client meeting.

04

Sector knowledge compounds across engagements

The knowledge graph from a fintech regulatory engagement persists. Three months later, on a related payments engagement, semantic retrieval surfaces relevant previous findings as grounding context. You're not researching from zero — you're building on what you already know.

05

Decision Log — evidence basis at delivery

When a deliverable is finalised, log the decision. Distilapp captures the full evidence state — every claim, its confidence score, every active contradiction, every known gap. The record you need when the client asks questions months later.
