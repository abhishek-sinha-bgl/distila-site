The problem

Fast research that
can't survive a challenge.

Generic AI tools produce fluent, confident answers. They don't tell you which sources conflict, which findings are contested, or whether the confidence is real. That's fine for a draft. It's a liability in client delivery.

Problem 01

Confidence with no basis

An AI says "revenue growth is accelerating." At what confidence? From which sources? Contested by whom? Without answers, you're relaying the model's opinion, not evidence. Partners and clients find out — often at the worst moment.

Problem 02

Every engagement starts from scratch

Your firm has done three engagements in the same sector. Every time, the research starts over. Institutional knowledge evaporates between projects. Distilapp's knowledge graph persists — sector intelligence compounds engagement by engagement.

Problem 03

Client confidentiality on cloud tools

NDA'd materials, client data, sensitive financials — none of it should be processed on a remote server with opaque data retention policies. Distilapp runs locally. Nothing leaves the machine.

Problem 04

No audit trail when scope is questioned

Six months after delivery, the client asks: what did you know when you made that recommendation? Generic tools have nothing. Distilapp's Decision Log has everything — timestamped, immutable, exportable.

How Distilapp works for you

From brief to
billable deliverable.

Faster research with traceable findings. Every engagement adds to a private knowledge graph your firm keeps — sector by sector, client by client.

01

Stake the research question — not just a topic

Distilapp works from specific research questions, not vague topics. "What is the regulatory risk profile for fintech lending in the EU in 2026?" produces targeted, scoped findings — not a general summary of fintech. The pipeline phases focus on what's actually relevant to the engagement question.

02

Every finding is a structured claim with a score

Not a paragraph. A typed object: status (Verified / Contested / Weak), confidence percentage, source tier, provider consensus count, citations. When a partner asks "how confident are we in this?" you have a precise, defensible answer — not "the AI seemed pretty sure."

03

Contested findings are flagged — not hidden

Conflicting analyst forecasts, divergent regulatory interpretations, competing market size estimates — Distilapp surfaces them as contested claims with both sides documented. You decide how to handle the conflict in the deliverable. You're not blindsided by it in the client meeting.

04

Sector knowledge compounds across engagements

The knowledge graph from a fintech regulatory engagement persists. Three months later, on a related payments engagement, Distilapp's vec0 search retrieves relevant previous findings as grounding context. You're not researching from zero — you're building on what the firm already knows.

05

Deliverable formats — board paper, investment memo, briefing note

The Publish flow generates the right format for the client context. Board paper: bottom line first, risk flagged, assumptions explicit. Investment memo: thesis, evidence, counterargument, confidence. Briefing note: findings, gaps, recommendations. Paste your firm's template and Distilapp adapts the output to your house style.
