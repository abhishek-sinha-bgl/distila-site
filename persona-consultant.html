The consulting problem

Four things a polished deck
usually cannot tell you later.

What evidence actually supported the claim?

A reference list is not enough. The relevant question is whether the cited material supports the exact proposition, under the conditions in which the recommendation was made.

Was the apparent consensus independent?

Ten articles can still trace back to one underlying study. Epistamate's target is provenance-aware corroboration, not counting URLs or model agreement.

What did the analyst actually review?

AI can prioritise attention. A reviewer should still be able to distinguish a model suggestion from a human inspection, disposition and authorisation.

What should survive into the next engagement?

Institutional knowledge should carry provenance, review history, applicability and recency — not merely old slides retrieved into another model context.

What Epistamate is built to add

Evidence that survives the read-out.
Research state that survives the engagement.

01

Claim-level evidence inspection

Consequential assertions remain addressable below the report level, with source identity, evidence posture, qualifications, contradictions and gaps available for inspection.

Client question: “What are we basing this on?” should lead to the evidence chain, not to a search through footnotes.
02

Reviewer provenance

AI triage and challenge can compress the workload, but explicit researcher actions are recorded separately. A senior reviewer can see what the system proposed and what the analyst actually reviewed.

Governance rule: human acceptance is important for accountability, but it does not directly make weak evidence stronger.
03

Known unknowns that remain visible

Gaps and contradictions survive synthesis rather than being rewritten into smooth prose. That gives the engagement team a defensible boundary around what the current evidence does and does not warrant.

Practical effect: uncertainty becomes something to manage and research further, not something to hide.
04

Institutional knowledge with revalidation

The target Knowledge layer carries prior evidence and review state forward, then checks applicability, recency and contradiction before reuse. The next engagement should start informed without inheriting yesterday's conclusion as automatic truth.

Important: durable cross-topic Knowledge compounding is still being rebuilt in the canonical product; it is a product direction, not a finished early-access claim.
