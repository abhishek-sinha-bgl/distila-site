The problem

Compliance built on
Word documents
and memory.

Most compliance knowledge lives in Word documents, email threads, and individual expertise. When the regulation updates, no one knows which documents need revising or which client positions are now wrong.

Problem 01

Regulatory change propagates invisibly

EBA publishes a consultation. ECB issues a supervisory expectation. FCA updates guidance. Each update potentially contradicts positions your team holds in other documents. Without a live contradiction engine, you find out from the client.

Problem 02

Client confidentiality rules out cloud tools

Matter-specific research, client correspondence, regulatory analysis — none of it belongs on a remote server. Distilapp is fully local. The knowledge base lives on your machine, not in a vendor's data centre.

Problem 03

AI tools state positions, not evidence

A generic AI says "under DORA, financial entities must..." It doesn't tell you the source was an interpretive note from 2023 that the EBA has since superseded. Distilapp cites the specific document, its date, and flags when newer authority exists.

Problem 04

No defensible record for advice given

The EU AI Act, GDPR enforcement decisions, and financial regulation all create liability for advice based on stale or incorrect interpretations. Distilapp's Decision Log records exactly what the evidence said at the moment advice was given.

How Distilapp works for you

From regulatory update
to client position.

Pre-loaded with FCA, EBA, ECB, SEC, EU Official Journal, and FDA feeds. Contradictions between new guidance and existing positions surface automatically.

01

Regulatory sources — pre-configured, updated daily

Distilapp ships with FCA Handbook updates, EBA regulatory publications, ECB supervisory communications, EU Official Journal, SEC releases, and FDA guidance as pre-configured Tier 1 sources. A local model filters and indexes new items daily at zero API cost. When DORA technical standards update, they're in your knowledge base before you've opened your email.

02

Regulatory Update lens — affected entities, effective dates, actions required

The Regulatory Update lens extracts structured findings from new guidance: the rule, the entities it applies to, the effective date, and the required action. Every update produces a typed claim with these fields — not a prose summary you have to re-parse later.

03

Contradiction detection — new rules vs existing positions

When new guidance contradicts a position in your knowledge graph, Distilapp flags it as a contested claim with both authorities named and the specific disagreement described. Triplet-based detection — not keyword matching — means "prohibition on X in context Y" is correctly matched against "permission for X under Z circumstances," not just word overlap.

04

Regulatory position tracker — rules mapped to client states

For each client or matter, maintain a position graph: which rules apply, what your current position is, the confidence in that position, and which positions are now contested by newer guidance. The graph persists across sessions — your compliance positions compound rather than reset.

05

Decision Log — advice with a timestamp and evidence basis

When advice is given or a position finalised, log the decision. Distilapp records every claim, the regulatory authority behind it, the effective date, and any active contradictions you were aware of. That record is the evidence trail that demonstrates due diligence — the kind EU AI Act enforcement decisions and professional liability cases increasingly require.
