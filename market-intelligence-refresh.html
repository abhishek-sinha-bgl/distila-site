Illustrative workflow · Governed market & competitive intelligence

You already have the research.
What could you discover next?

Strategy and marketing teams may already have substantial analyst research, spending intelligence, competitive scans and industry experience. What new questions, opportunities or decisions could that accumulated understanding support if its insights remained connected over time?

A proposed Epistamate workflow would pair Frontier AI investigation with preserved evidence, assumptions and professional judgement. New research might suggest a segment worth exploring, connect a market trend to an account question, or challenge an earlier assessment. The reasoning stays inspectable, and a new possibility does not automatically become an accepted recommendation.

An evolving analytical position

Put accumulated research
to work on new questions.

The opportunity is not to replace an analyst subscription, a BI dashboard or a skilled market research team. It is to connect the knowledge they generate across industry, segment and company questions, so emerging insights can be investigated against an evidence-linked understanding of what was established before.

Existing intelligence

Start from what you know

Keep segment definitions, market assessments, source limitations, open questions and professional decisions connected, rather than treating each new investigation as unrelated.

Fresh connections

What else could matter?

Combine new public developments with authorized internal and subscription research to propose relevant new questions. Check source identity, dates, coverage and applicability before relying on them.

Professional judgement

Which ideas warrant action?

Explore promising hypotheses, affected assumptions and downstream decisions. Preserve earlier reasoning; model-generated possibilities stay provisional until the professional accepts or qualifies them.

Start with the team's ambition: “We have valuable industry studies and IT-spending intelligence, but we cannot consistently investigate what they might imply for adjacent segments and target companies.” A proposed Epistamate workflow could help professionals carry forward prior assessments, ask new Frontier AI-assisted questions and inspect the evidence behind emerging possibilities. The business test: would a better-informed segment or market priority decision matter enough to justify maintaining this analytical state compared with the team's existing research tools? A promising lead is not yet an opportunity or a validated result.

Evidence Room · Authorised research and data

A new analyst report arrives.
What should we explore now?

A firm might subscribe to industry analyst libraries (for example, services used to access Gartner-, IDC- or other third-party research), or an IT-services spend database exposed through Power BI. It may contain estimated company-by-company spending, vendor shares, sector comparisons or very long market reports not available on the public web. These are potentially useful sources—not automatically verified truths, freely reusable content or implied Epistamate integrations.

01 · Authorized intake

Keep what was actually supplied

A professional might add a permitted PDF, analyst-report extract, data export, citation record or internal note to an Evidence Room. Capture source identity, publisher, edition, relevant sections, as-of date, licensing conditions and access restrictions.

02 · Inspect evidence and relevance

Read the important part

Compare new material with existing segment assumptions and known gaps. A long report may require bounded section-by-section analysis; tabular estimates, charts and quadrants need careful interpretation, categories, dates and precise source support.

03 · Propose impacts for review

Reopen only what is affected

Flag positions potentially strengthened, weakened, qualified or made stale, including downstream account or market recommendations. Do not silently revise accepted state: explain the candidate change and let the professional approve, defer or reject it.

Illustrative result: An authorized IT-spend dataset suggests that a target account allocates a larger estimated share of spending to an incumbent vendor than previously assumed. That might alter a market-access hypothesis or prompt a discovery question. It does not prove a current contract, customer dissatisfaction, available budget or a sales opportunity without corroboration.

Licensing, access and confidentiality: Subscriptions to analyst libraries or data vendors do not themselves authorize redistribution, model uploads, retention of full text, or reuse across accounts. Use only permitted content and processing arrangements, including approved extracts or human-authored descriptions where full-file ingestion is not allowed. Epistamate does not currently claim direct connections to proprietary research databases, Power BI, or other enterprise intelligence services.

Product maturity: The Research Workspace already has an Evidence Room for durable research inputs. The integrated, license-aware, cross-market intake and automated impact-assessment workflow described here is a potential adaptation, not an implemented end-to-end market intelligence product.

Illustrative quarterly reassessment · invented example

New evidence need not produce
a completely new thesis.

Consider a strategy team comparing two hypothetical service-market segments and revisiting earlier priorities.

Earlier accepted judgement

Segment A ranked first

The team favored Segment A based on a recorded combination of demand evidence, buyer access and delivery feasibility. Segment B carried unresolved regulatory and adoption assumptions.

Incoming licensed and public material

Different source roles

A newly permitted research extract revises an estimated spending pattern, while a competitor announcement makes a claim about Segment A. The dataset estimate and vendor claim have different authority and limitations.

Candidate affected findings

Selective follow-up

Check source comparability and revisit the relevant commercial assumptions; test whether the competitive proposition still holds. Keep unaffected assessments unchanged unless evidence warrants review.

Possible next insight: “The earlier segment preference remains the accepted baseline, but estimated spending and competitive differentiation require re-examination before renewing this recommendation.” This is a candidate attention signal, not a validated market conclusion or automatic business decision.

Industry → Segment → Client

Reuse reasoning.
Do not transfer conclusions blindly.

An insight drawn from a licensed report, external industry development or a successful client intervention may be relevant to several business decisions. But the analysis must preserve the origin, sharing restrictions and conditions under which it is applicable.

Industry-level evidence

Common external context

One sourced development may support a shared industry hypothesis, subject to access rights, source identity and applicability limits.

Segment-level interpretation

Method-specific meaning

The same development could have different implications for different customer segments or market-entry scenarios. Interpretive judgements remain explicit.

Account-level decision

A new hypothesis, not a fact

Account A's success does not prove Account B needs the same intervention. Test local conditions, client priorities and permissible use of evidence before recommending action.

Where the proposed value lies

From valuable research
to new, defensible possibilities.

Existing analyst subscriptions, search, BI and Frontier AI already provide information and analysis. The proposed differentiator is the governed state connecting each assessment to the evidence that justified it, later contradictions, reviewer decisions and the next consequential question.

Persistent analytical state

Keep the “why”

Know which sources, assumptions and method supported an earlier industry or segment diagnosis—and where its limits remain.

Selective impact assessment

Focus scarce attention

Surface candidate changes that matter to an existing hypothesis or recommendation, not an undifferentiated feed of newly published reports.

Professionally authorised output

Inform the next decision

Prepare candidate changes, questions and relevant evidence for existing strategy briefs, market plans or account discussions. Final decisions and disclosure remain with the professional.

Adoption test: If existing analysts and tools already maintain decisions, evidence, applicability and refreshes with little friction, another layer may add limited value. The architecture is potentially worthwhile where preserved reasoning materially improves continuing or cross-context decisions—not simply because it can generate another report.

Questions and boundaries

A governed foundation,
not an undisclosed analyst-data feed.

Can the service automatically search our subscription research library or Power BI spending database?

That functionality is not offered in this market intelligence scenario. Any future integration would require explicit authorization, technical capability and appropriate licensing. A permitted manual extract, source record or summary may be the only appropriate input.

What happens with a 200-page analyst report or a market quadrant?

Length and layout create risks of omissions and misinterpretation. A responsible workflow would identify the exact edition and relevant pages, selectively analyze content, verify tables/graphics against their source and limitations, and keep a reviewer in the loop. A third-party quadrant is not an authoritative assessment without confirming publisher, category, period and usage rights.

Will a new report automatically update approved recommendations?

No. A candidate impact can prompt targeted reconsideration, but the prior accepted conclusion remains traceable. Any revised assessment requires an authorized human disposition. The proposed account/market impact pathway is not represented as an operational automated service.

Why not use Frontier AI plus our existing research portal?

That may be enough. Epistamate would need to justify maintaining evidence-linked hypotheses, decision rationale and cross-iteration impact relationships over time. Its potential contribution is governed analytical continuity, not proprietary research access or inherently better answers to one-off questions.

For implemented research components and controlled evidence examples, see the Governed Research Workspace and research examples. No unattended analyst-feed integration, proprietary-content reuse entitlement or market-intelligence performance benefit is being claimed.
