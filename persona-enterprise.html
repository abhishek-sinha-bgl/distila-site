Why the workflow is different

Evidence quality should be
inspectable, not implied.

Evidence state, not file storage

The target is to preserve why a claim was believed, what evidence supported it, what contradicted it and when it was last revalidated.

Human and AI actions separated

Model suggestions, researcher decisions and evidence authority remain distinct so governance does not reduce to “a human was in the loop”.

Local-first current product

The current product is a Windows desktop application with local canonical persistence and explicit provider/network permissions, not a finished team/cloud platform.

Institutional Knowledge is a roadmap pillar

Same-topic continuation and reassessment exist; durable cross-topic canonical Knowledge is being rebuilt and is not claimed as complete today.
