The problem

Licensed intelligence
that sits unused.

The average enterprise research team has access to multiple analyst databases, proprietary feeds, and internal knowledge repositories. Most of that content never makes it into actual research decisions in a structured, scored, traceable way.

Problem 01

Gartner reports in a shared drive — not a pipeline

Your Gartner subscription produces excellent reports. They go into a shared drive. Analysts read them, extract informal notes, and those insights evaporate. Distilapp ingests them, extracts claims, scores them, and makes them searchable across every future research run.

Problem 02

Cloud research tools can't reach your data

Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT — none of them can reach your VPN-gated analyst portals, your intranet research libraries, or your authenticated licensed databases. Distilapp runs on your team's machines, inside your network perimeter, with the same access as their browsers.

Problem 03

Team knowledge doesn't accumulate

Analyst A researches the competitive landscape in Q1. Analyst B starts a related engagement in Q3 with no access to what A found. Shared knowledge graphs mean the team's intelligence compounds — every analyst's research adds to the same evidence base.

Problem 04

No governance layer for AI-assisted research

The EU AI Act and corporate governance standards are moving toward requiring documented evidence of what AI systems knew and how confident they were. Distilapp's Decision Log and audit export give compliance and legal teams the record they need — built into the research workflow, not retrofitted.

How Distilapp works for teams

Licensed databases.
Shared graphs.
Governance built in.

Team Starter and Professional tiers share knowledge graphs, source libraries, and admin controls. Enterprise adds SSO, on-prem deployment, audit log export, and SLA.

01

Licensed databases connected — any portal your browser can reach

Any URL a team member's browser can reach on your network, Distilapp can ingest. Configure authenticated portals once in Settings — Distilapp uses existing browser sessions, not separate credentials. Licensed analyst reports, contract databases, intranet research hubs — all become sources in the shared evidence pipeline. Research assets your organisation already paid for, finally in the workflow.

02

Source library — team-wide, refreshed by local model

Pre-configured Tier 1 feeds — RAND, IMF, NBER, arXiv, Brookings, sector-specific sources — refresh daily via a local model that filters, summarises, and indexes only what's relevant, at zero API cost. Add authenticated data APIs (Refinitiv, Semantic Scholar, GDELT) with your team's keys. Every analyst starts from a shared, curated, always-fresh evidence base.

03

Shared knowledge graph — team intelligence compounds

Team Starter and above share the knowledge graph across seats. Analyst A's entity extraction from a Q1 competitive landscape study is available to Analyst B in Q3. The graph grows with the team — sector by sector, engagement by engagement. When a new analyst joins, they don't start from zero; they start from everything the team has already learned.

04

Multi-LLM pipeline — configured for your team's providers

Connect your organisation's preferred AI providers — Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex, or local models for sensitive research. The pipeline assigns the best model to each role: fast models for research workers, frontier models for orchestration and synthesis. Bring your enterprise API contracts — Distilapp uses them.

05

Governance export — audit trail for compliance and legal

Every Decision Log is exportable in a structured format your compliance team can work with. The full evidence state at decision time — claims, confidence scores, source citations, active contradictions, knowledge gaps. The documentation that EU AI Act high-risk use cases, financial services regulators, and corporate governance standards are increasingly requiring.

06

On-prem and air-gapped deployment for sensitive environments

For teams handling classified, legally privileged, or commercially sensitive research — Distilapp can run fully air-gapped with local Ollama models. No data leaves the network. The knowledge graph, the evidence pipeline, the source library — all local. Enterprise tier includes dedicated onboarding and SLA for these deployments.
