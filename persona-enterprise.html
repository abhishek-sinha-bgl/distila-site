The problem

Licensed intelligence that sits unused.

The average enterprise research team has access to multiple analyst databases, proprietary feeds, and internal knowledge repositories. Most of that content never makes it into actual research decisions in a structured, scored, traceable way.

Problem 01
Gartner reports in a shared drive — not a pipeline
Your Gartner subscription produces excellent reports. They go into a shared drive. Analysts read them, extract informal notes, and those insights evaporate. Distilapp ingests them, extracts claims, scores them, and makes them searchable across every future research run.
Problem 02
Cloud research tools can't reach your data
Perplexity, Gemini, ChatGPT — none of them can reach your VPN-gated analyst portals, your intranet research libraries, or your authenticated licensed databases. Distilapp runs on your team's machines, inside your network perimeter, with the same access as their browsers.
Problem 03
Team knowledge doesn't accumulate
Analyst A researches the competitive landscape in Q1. Analyst B starts a related engagement in Q3 with no access to what A found. A shared knowledge base means the team's intelligence compounds — every analyst's work adds to the same evidence base.
Problem 04
No governance layer for AI-assisted research
EU AI Act and corporate governance standards are moving toward requiring documented evidence of what AI systems knew and how confident they were. Distilapp's Decision Log and audit export give compliance teams the record they need — built into the research workflow, not retrofitted.
How it works for teams

Licensed databases. Shared knowledge. Governance built in.

01

Licensed databases connected — any portal your browser can reach

Any URL a team member's browser can reach on your network, Distilapp can ingest. Configure authenticated portals once in Settings — Distilapp uses existing browser sessions, not separate credentials. Licensed analyst reports, contract databases, intranet research hubs — all become sources in the evidence pipeline.

02

Shared knowledge base — within your perimeter

When research is finished, publish it to the shared knowledge base — a self-hosted database on your own infrastructure. Verified claims only. Read-only for everyone. Searchable across all published briefs. Every analyst's work builds the organisational intelligence asset that new team members inherit.

03

Multi-LLM pipeline — configured for your providers

Connect your organisation's preferred AI providers — Anthropic, Azure OpenAI, Google Vertex, or local models for sensitive research. The pipeline assigns the best model to each role. Bring your enterprise API contracts — Distilapp uses them.

04

Governance export — audit trail for compliance and legal

Every Decision Log is exportable in a structured format your compliance team can work with. The full evidence state at decision time — claims, confidence scores, source citations, active contradictions, knowledge gaps. The documentation that EU AI Act high-risk use cases increasingly require.

05

Fully within your perimeter — including air-gapped

For teams handling sensitive research, Distilapp can run fully air-gapped with local Ollama models. No data leaves the network. The knowledge graph, the evidence pipeline, the shared knowledge base — all inside your perimeter, under your control.
