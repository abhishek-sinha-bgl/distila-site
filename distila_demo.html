About this demo: This demonstration uses the Anthropic API to simulate the Distilapp DCBR pipeline — showing how claims are extracted, scored against evidence-quality criteria, challenged adversarially, and compiled into a structured brief with gap tracking. In the full desktop application, this runs locally with multi-provider parallel retrieval, a persistent knowledge graph, and an immutable Decision Log. Evidence scores shown reflect the formula structure; in production, source tier assignments draw on real document ingestion and retrieval.
