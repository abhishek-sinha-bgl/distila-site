Not a prettier research report. A research state you can reopen, challenge, continue and defend.

Frontier models can plan, search, read difficult material and challenge a draft. Epistamate keeps the evidence state, unresolved questions and researcher decisions outside the model so later work can be inspected and continued rather than reconstructed. Human review controls admission, not truth: accepting a proposition does not make weak evidence stronger.

These examples describe observed Workbench behaviour during product validation. They are not claims of comparative accuracy, productivity improvement or client outcomes.

Document-led verification · Industry fraud/cyber paper

Route reachability, citation identity and claim support stayed separate.

A document-led run parsed and checked 27 citations from a supplied paper. Ten citation routes were reachable; 17 failed or were blocked. Epistamate did not treat either state as a verdict on the nearby claim.

The run ultimately analysed 11 unique preserved source contents after deterministic content-hash de-duplication. Its accepted position separated supportable underlying mechanisms from weaker quantitative claims, citation-integrity problems and unresolved identity/support questions.

Question-led research · AI coding-assistant productivity

A partial corpus remained a partial corpus.

In a controlled first-pass run, 14 planned targets produced 12 acquired sources while 2 were explicitly unresolved or deferred. Forty-one candidate claims were admitted for analysis.

The synthesis returned 8 findings, 5 Socratic challenges, 7 divergence diagnoses, 11 knowledge gaps and 5 validation leads. Step 6 preserved 20 follow-on agenda items rather than presenting the first pass as finished research.

Why this matters: the experiment is not whether an LLM can write a fluent research report. It is whether incomplete acquisition, citation-integrity uncertainty, competing evidence and future validation needs remain reconstructable after synthesis.
