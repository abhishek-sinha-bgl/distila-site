Epistamate Research · Windows desktop · Early access

Use frontier intelligence to research harder.
Keep evidence authority outside the model.

Not a prettier research report. A research state you can reopen, challenge, continue and defend.

Frontier models can plan, search, read difficult material and challenge a draft. Epistamate keeps the evidence state, unresolved questions and researcher decisions outside the model so later work can be inspected and continued rather than reconstructed.

Support

A real citation can still fail the claim.

Keep the exact proposition tied to the material that actually supports, qualifies or contradicts it.

Independence

Forty URLs may still be one result.

Shared work, dataset and source-family lineage should not silently become corroboration.

Applicability

Strong evidence may not transfer.

Population, jurisdiction, configuration, task and time constrain what a finding warrants here.

Contradiction

Disagreement stays visible.

Counterevidence, qualifications and gaps remain research objects instead of being smoothed away.

Researcher review

AI priority is not human review.

The record separates what a model suggested from what a researcher actually inspected and decided.

Continuation

Prior evidence is revalidated.

Same-topic research can deepen without rewriting history or automatically inheriting answer authority.
