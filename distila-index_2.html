The problem

AI gave you more answers.
It gave you less certainty.

The information keeps coming. The confidence keeps dropping. Here's why.

01

Everything is plausible, nothing is provable

ChatGPT, Perplexity, Deep Research — all return fluent sentences with no indication of what's verified, contested, or simply fabricated. You spend more time checking than deciding.

02

Every session forgets everything

Research you did last Tuesday is invisible today. There's no accumulation, no linkage between topics, no way to ask "what do I already know about this?" Knowledge evaporates.

03

One output, regardless of what you need

An analyst, an executive, and a first-year student all need the same facts framed completely differently. A single chat output serves none of them well.

04

No audit trail for your decisions

When a choice is challenged six months later, you have no record of what you knew, how confident you were, or what contradictions you were aware of at the time.

How Distila works

Four phases. One growing intelligence layer.

Every research run goes through the same rigorous pipeline — then adds to a private knowledge graph that compounds over time.

Phase 1

Discovery scan

Distila quickly maps the terrain — themes, initial claims, and knowledge gaps — using your own LLM providers in parallel. Web grounding optional, always transparent.

Phase 2

Deep research

Multiple AI providers work in parallel. Every claim extracted carries a status (Verified, Contested, Weak), a confidence score, a source tier, and the reasoning chain behind it.

Phase 3

Socratic challenge

The system interrogates its own findings. What assumptions underpin each claim? Where does the evidence actually conflict? What would have to be false for this conclusion to hold?

Phase 4

Synthesis for your lens

The same evidence base becomes an Analyst brief, an Executive summary, a Financial note, or a Sales intelligence report — shaped for how you actually make decisions.

Always on

Knowledge graph

The configurable pipeline

Your models. Your rules.
Every role, your choice.

Distila's pipeline assigns a specific role to each model — so the best model for research isn't forced to write, and the best model for writing isn't wasted on verification. Mix any combination. Switch providers per task. Run fully airgapped if required.

Our philosophy
Responsible AI by design
AI should do the heavy lifting.
Humans should own every conclusion.

Responsible AI isn't a compliance checkbox. It's an architecture decision. Distila is built from the ground up with the human governance layer that AI tools almost always skip — because that's what makes the difference between a tool that impresses and one you can actually trust.
