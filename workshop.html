EPISTAMATE · ORGANISATIONAL INTELLIGENCE WORKSHOP

Work on a real organisational question.
See the thinking evolve.

A practical experience for a cross-functional leadership team working on its own organisational question. Participants use a personal app, challenge one another's assumptions and see the group's reasoning develop across sessions.

A research-informed method and Epistamate's evidence-and-reasoning kernel support the work. Participants learn by practising, not by listening to a course about AI.

A practical example · synthetic scenario

A possibility becomes a better question.

Suppose a company asks whether customers' AI agents could help them discover its offerings. Product, commercial and legal teams see different opportunities and constraints. Rather than vote on the idea, they investigate what would make it worth testing.

INITIAL POSSIBILITY

Customers' AI agents could discover our offerings.

An imaginative proposition that the group is free to explore, without pretending it is a market forecast.

DIFFERENT PERSPECTIVES

Useful challenges enter.

Product asks about machine-readable information. Commercial asks whether customers actually want it. Legal asks who authorised the agent to transact.

NEXT SESSION'S BRIEF

What needs reconsideration?

Agent identity does not establish customer authority. Demand and transaction permission are distinct questions. Both may matter before a bounded trial.

This illustration draws on a synthetic workshop evaluation. It does not represent an actual customer engagement, verified market demand or a recommendation to launch a product.

The method and the foundation

Research-informed judgement.
Proportionate assurance.

A method shaped by research

The facilitation draws on research into organisational learning, evidence quality, human-AI judgement and constructive challenge. Executives practise a method they can use again. Explore the research and essays →

Epistamate underneath

Epistamate's kernel supports attributed contributions, candidate interpretations, reviewed reasoning and change history. Model-assisted synthesis does not independently verify organisational assertions or approve decisions. How the foundation works →

Blue-sky ideas remain welcome. The key safeguard is faithful relationships, not citations for every speculation. Quick Pass is intended to let the facilitator accept a reasonable provisional synthesis while inspecting uncertain or consequential links more closely. A short list of important open questions is more useful than an exhaustive warning register.

Format and outcomes

A guided journey, with learning built into the work.

The five-session programme moves from open possibilities through enabling conditions and challenges to reassessment. A shorter intensive format is also being evaluated. Facilitator-led recaps make the learning cumulative.

GROUP REASONING JOURNEY↗

What did the organisation explore?

Possibilities considered, perspectives that changed them, unresolved differences and three or four consequential questions to investigate next.

Shared learning, with provenance and review context
PERSONALISED LEARNING REPORT↗

What did I learn from the exercise?

Your ideas, the questions you challenged, relevant private reflections and ways to apply the method in your own work.

Private reflections remain personal

These are intended report formats under refinement, not downloadable outputs from an existing customer engagement. External intelligence and delivery arrangements are tailored to the participating organisation.

The promise is practical learning: participants learn to explore a real organisational question more thoughtfully. There is no guaranteed transformation strategy, approved investment case or organisation-of-the-future blueprint after five sessions.
